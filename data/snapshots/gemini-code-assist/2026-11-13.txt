Gemini Code Assist Standard and Enterprise subscriptions
Stay organized with collections
Save and categorize content based on your preferences.
Starting October 9, 2026, you can no longer purchase new Gemini Code Assist Standard or Enterprise subscriptions. Additionally, new Gemini Enterprise Standard and Plus subscriptions no longer include an entitlement to Gemini Code Assist.
Your existing subscriptions will continue to have access to Gemini Code Assist until the following dates:
Existing Gemini Code Assist subscriptions will remain supported until the end of your contractual agreement.
If you have an annual subscription, you can continue to
auto-renew your subscription
until January 31, 2027. After this date, annual subscriptions can't be renewed.
If you have a monthly subscription, you can continue
auto-renew your subscription
until December 31, 2027. After this date, monthly subscriptions can't be renewed.
Existing Gemini Enterprise subscriptions will retain the their Gemini Code Assist entitlement until March 31, 2027.
Going forward, agentic coding assistance will be available through
Antigravity
, which is included in
Gemini Enterprise editions
. You should plan your subscription transition to Gemini Enterprise before your plan reaches its final renewal or contract end date.
FAQs
Where can I find more information about Antigravity IDEs?
See
Antigravity IDE extensions
, which includes extensions for VS Code and JetBrains.
How do I migrate from Gemini CLI to Antigravity CLI?
See our
migration guide
.
Can I still add licenses to my existing Gemini Code Assist subscription?
You can continue to
add licenses
until January 31, 2027. After this date, no new licenses can be added.
What models are supported for Gemini Code Assist?
Support will be limited to the Gemini 3.x model family only.
