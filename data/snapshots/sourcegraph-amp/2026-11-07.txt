Questions & Answers
Can I use Amp without a monthly tier?
Yes. Hobby has no monthly fee, and you can use your own model subscription or key and buy Amp credits as needed. An Amp paid tier gives you a big discount on orbs and includes Amp credits that you can use on any model.
What am I paying for?
A paid Amp tier includes orbin' time (minutes of orbs, our machines in the cloud that run your agents) and Amp credits (which you can use for inference on all models).
What is an orb, and how do orb minutes work?
An orb is a cloud computer where Amp can work remotely. We count minutes while an orb is running and stop when it automatically pauses while idle.
Self-hosted orbs, which run on your own infrastructure, are free on Hobby, Individual, and Teams and don't use orb minutes.
Are there any fees for using my own subscriptions or keys?
For Hobby, Individual, and Teams, when using your own subscription or key, there are no Amp token fees or limits. Your model provider's charges and limits still apply, of course. For Enterprise, custom pricing applies.
Does creating a workspace cost extra?
No. A team workspace has no extra fee, and each member can stay on Hobby or use a paid tier. Some workspace features, such as SAML/OIDC SSO, require the workspace to have at least one member on a paid tier.
What happens when my orb minutes run out?
You can keep using Amp by upgrading to a higher paid tier or buying more credits. See Orb pricing.
How do I cancel?
Cancel any time in your user settings. Your paid tier stays active until the end of its current monthly period.
Is there an Education Discount?
Students and teachers can get Megawatt for $10 per month. Learn about the Education Discount.
What kinds of data retention and security commitments do I get?
All users and workspaces, not just enterprise, are covered by our zero and minimal data retention policy. See our Security Reference for more information, including about our SOC 2 Type 2 certification.
