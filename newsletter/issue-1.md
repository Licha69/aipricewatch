# AI Price Watch #1: What got pricier in AI coding tools

*Draft, not sent. Every number below comes from an official pricing page, either an archived copy on the Wayback Machine or our own snapshot on 2026-10-16. Links go to the archived source. Prices in USD unless noted.*

Hi! This is the first AI Price Watch. Each week: what changed in the prices of AI coding and agent tools, with dates and sources. This first issue looks back at 2024–2026.

## The big picture
The $10–$20 starter plan is still around. The real change is at the top: almost every tool now has a **$100–$200/month** tier.

- **Cursor** added Ultra at **$200/mo** (seen 2025-07-29) and Pro+ at **$60/mo** (seen 2025-09-22).
- **ChatGPT** added Pro at **$200/mo** (seen 2025-03-04). Today's page shows Pro at **$100/mo** and a new Go plan at **$8/mo**.
- **Claude** added Max **from $100/mo** (seen 2025-05-01).
- **GitHub Copilot** added Pro+ at **$39/mo** (seen 2025-05-01). Today's page also lists Max at **$100/mo**.
- **Windsurf** today lists Max at **$200/mo**.

## Plans that went up
- **Windsurf (formerly Codeium) Pro: $10 → $15 → $20/mo.** $10 in Nov 2024, $15 by Jan 2025, $20 on today's page. Teams went from $12/seat (2024) to $80/mo per team + $40 per seat today.
- **Replit Core: $15 → $20/mo** between the Mar and May 2025 captures. Core now comes with $25/mo of usage credits, and you pay as you go after that. Today Replit also lists Pro at **$100/mo**.
- **Lovable** dropped its $20 Starter and moved to **Pro at $25/mo** (seen 2025-05-08). Business at $50/mo followed.

## Things to watch
- **Cursor's pricing page changed a lot this month.** On 2026-10-16 it showed Plus $8, Premium $30, Super $100, Ultra $200 and Teams $30/user. The 2026-09-02 capture showed $20 Individual and $40 Teams. We're double-checking this before calling it a price cut.
- **Seat vs usage pricing:** Claude Team now has Standard ($25/mo) and Premium ($125/mo) seats. Augment, Warp and Zed now sell plans as "$X of usage included".

## Got cheaper (or did it?)
- **ChatGPT Pro: $200 → $100/mo** between the 2025-09-04 capture and today. We'll check whether it's the same plan.

## Steady
- **Bolt:** Pro $25/mo and Teams $30/member, unchanged in every capture we checked (Apr–Oct 2026).
- **GitHub Copilot Pro** has stayed at **$10/mo** since 2024.
